The Baroque style of Salzburg

I traveled to Salzburg with my family in February 2025. An historic town celebrated for being the birthplace of Mozart with familiar sites used in the film "The Sound of Music," such as The Mirabell Palace, known for its grand interior Baroque staircase with puttis along the railing.
