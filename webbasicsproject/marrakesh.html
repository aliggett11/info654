Moorish style of Marrakesh

I traveled to Marrakesh, Morocco in April 2017 during my study abroad program in Florence.

We rode camels, explored the Bahia Palace, and shopped in the marketplace.

Our accommodation was a beautiful traditional riad with a central open air courtyard in the middle of the house.
