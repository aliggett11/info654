A little about my background

I'm Alexandra and I love to travel! I am originally from Nashville, TN, and have lived in New York for 12 years. My interest in traveling stemmed from the values instilled by my parents to stay curious about the world, as well as my love of art history and architecture. Noticing the architecture of a new city is the first way of immersing myself into the experience of travel. This website highlights the different architectural wonders I've encountered throughout three cities I've visited.

My studies

I studied art history, psychology, literature, and dance at NYU and am currently in the Masters of Library and Information Science program at Pratt, concentrating in archives. I value the process of photography as documentation of my life. All of the photographs on this site are my own.

I studied abroad in Florence, Italy during my junior year at NYU, which allowed me to take advantage of living in Europe for a semester and travel to 6 countries and around 25 cities.

What's next?

In November 2026 I will be traveling to Brazil for my friend's wedding. I will be staying in Rio de Janeiro and Aracaju. This will be my first time in South America.
